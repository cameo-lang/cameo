## Obstacles
- **Issue:** An exception, a `break` or a `retry` out of the middle of an
  expression leaves operands on the value stack.
  **Resolution:** BEGIN, WHILE and the `defined?` instructions remember the
  height of the stack they started at and cut it back.
- **Issue:** A rescue clause that names no class rescues StandardError,
  which is a fact of the source, not of the classes evaluated: `rescue
  *[]` names classes and rescues nothing.
  **Resolution:** The classes list of such a clause is empty, with no
  instruction, where one that names classes leaves a list.

## Outcome
What is left for the IR are definitions, `super`, `with`, `async` and
`await`. The pattern matcher exists twice, on the AST and on the IR, until
the tree walker goes.
