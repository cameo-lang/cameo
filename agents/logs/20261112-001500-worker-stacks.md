# Task: workers stop at a pause, and the dump holds their stacks

**Started:** 2026-11-11 23:40:00
**Ended:** 2026-11-12 00:15:00
**Strategy:** Feature (TDD)
**Status:** Completed
**Complexity:** Simple
**Used Models:** Fable 5.2
**Token usage (Estimated):** 600k input, 35k output

## Objective
The second part of step 25 of `doc/design/ir.md`: a pause no longer waits
for the calls `async` started. A worker stops where it is, the dump holds
its stack, and the resumed run has it go on.

## Progress
- [x] `dumpStack`, `restoreShells` and `restoreStack` work on one
      interpreter's stack (`stackMsg`), the script's or a worker's
- [x] A worker freezes before an instruction as the script does;
      `asyncCall.run` and `finish` keep its promise pending
- [x] `pauser.need`: a waiter that cannot pause marks its promise needed;
      the worker does not freeze, or is thawed
- [x] Runners take no queued call while a pause is asked for
- [x] `Interp.quiet`, and `dumpPromises`: a call is settled, queued
      (`Dump.Call`) or frozen (`Dump.Worker`)
- [x] `Interp.wake`: `Run` on a resumed program queues the calls and
      starts the workers; `Run` on a paused one thaws them
- [x] Tests: a worker paused inside a loop, a run that goes on with a
      stopped worker, a needed worker that runs to its end, a tree of
      workers awaiting workers paused at every leaf; `-race`

## Obstacles
- **Issue:** The design had a worker park with its goroutine and freeze
  when `Dump` said so. A call the awaiter runs itself, on its own
  goroutine, would have parked the awaiter with it, and nothing would
  have called `Dump`.
  **Resolution:** A worker freezes at once and gives up its goroutine,
  and is thawed on a new one when something needs its promise. The
  design text says so now.
- **Issue:** The race detector found a worker running on two goroutines:
  it was in the table of frozen workers as soon as it decided to
  freeze, and a thaw could start it while its old goroutine was still
  returning through `start`.
  **Resolution:** A worker is frozen for the others only when `finish`
  says so, the last thing its goroutine does. A need that arrived
  meanwhile has the worker go on right there.
- **Issue:** A test of `cameo inspect` expected the two calls of its
  script settled in the dump. They are now settled or pending, as a
  worker had run them or not.
  **Resolution:** The test takes either for each.
- **Issue:** Starting the workers in `Resume` gave them a top level
  without the modules a host adds after `Resume`.
  **Resolution:** `Run` starts them.

## Outcome
A pause is quick whatever a script's workers are doing, unless something
that cannot pause waits for one. Left of the step's design: nothing.
A worker's top level after a resume is the resumed run's, which
`doc/design/pause.md` says.
