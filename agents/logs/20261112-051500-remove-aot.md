## Objective
`cameo build` goes: it compiled pure methods over Integers and Floats to
Go and left the rest of a script to the interpreter. The decision
followed a comparison of the evaluators and a look at what the compiler
covers: an interpreter stays in any case, for a paused run, a proc from
another program, irb and the debugger, and a complete compile would
conflict with pausing.

Two pull requests: this one removes the compiler and what exists for it;
the next removes what the evaluator keeps for compiled methods.
