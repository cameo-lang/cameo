rat — Rational and Complex

cameo has no Rational or Complex. The checker refuses the literals 1r and 2i, and Rational(), to_r and to_c do not exist.

What cameo refuses

A rational or imaginary literal is refused before the script runs; a call of Rational fails when it is reached.

third = 1/3r
script.cameo:1: rational literals are not supported (see https://15b.it/rat)
p Rational(1, 3)
script.cameo:1: undefined method 'Rational' for main (NoMethodError)

Why

Every value a cameo script holds can be written as a protobuf message, for a dump, a worker or a host. Integer, Float, String and the collections each have a form there; Rational and Complex would each need one of their own, with their arithmetic, for numbers few scripts use. They were left out to keep the core small.

What to write instead

Integer arithmetic stays exact, fdiv divides into a Float, and BigDecimal computes with decimals as exactly as asked.

require "bigdecimal"

p 1.fdiv(3) + 1.fdiv(6)
p (BigDecimal("1") / 3 + BigDecimal("1") / 6).round(10).to_s
